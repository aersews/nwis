WELL: WELL-017

FORMATION: Formation-Y

DEPTH: 2826

EVENT: STUCK PIPE

SEVERITY: SEVERE

DATE: 2019-08-14

DRILLING OBSERVATION:

While drilling through Formation-Y at approximately 2826 m MD,
the operation experienced increasing torque followed by inability
to rotate and reciprocate the drill string normally.

The drilling team stopped drilling and circulated the well.

MITIGATION:

The team worked the pipe, circulated at controlled flow rate,
and performed backreaming operations.

The pipe was successfully freed after approximately 37 minutes.

LESSON LEARNED:

Monitor torque and drag closely while drilling through this
interval. Similar behaviour may indicate elevated stuck-pipe risk.
